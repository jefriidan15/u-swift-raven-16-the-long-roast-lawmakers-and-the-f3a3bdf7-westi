One satirical sentence about lawmakers who regulate AI, crypto or the
internet but cannot convert a file to PDF, for the make.fun bounty
[u-swift-raven-16-the-long-roast-lawmakers-and-the-f3a3bdf7](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-raven-16-the-long-roast-lawmakers-and-the-f3a3bdf7).
Entry: one sentence, at most 320 characters, punch on the last words.

The entry program writes a single sentence (at most 320 characters) to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> The senator who wrote the bill to regulate artificial intelligence has
> been trying to convert his own speech to PDF since Tuesday, and his
> office now lists it as a pending infrastructure project.

Run with Node 22+: `node main.ts`
